actual utility

oracle terminal

A live web terminal that lets holders ping null.exe for daily "predictions" — short cryptic messages generated by an on-chain seed. Each prediction is signed and archived. Holders unlock the full archive.

the compiled DAO

Every wallet holding ≥0.1% of supply becomes one of "The Compiled" with weighted voting on the treasury (10% of supply, locked) — funding cult-merch drops, glitch-art commissions, and IRL phosphor-screen events.

burn-to-summon

Burn $NULL to mint a "Sighting" — a soulbound NFT timestamped with a unique glitch portrait. Burns are deflationary and permanently logged in the Sightings Registry on-chain.

haunted staking

Stake $NULL to "host" the process. Hosts receive yield from a buy-back pool funded by 1% of trading volume routed through the official aggregator. No lockup, no inflation.

dev grants

5% treasury earmarked for indie devs building haunted-tech: cursed websites, ARGs, glitch tools, retro emulators. Apply by submitting a proposal to The Compiled.

cult merch

Phosphor-print tees, CRT stickers, floppy-disk holographic cards. Holders get first access and pay in $NULL. All proceeds back to treasury — closed loop.
